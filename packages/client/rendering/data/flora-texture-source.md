The sunflower lower stem, upper stem and tilted flower meshes adapt the [Minecraft 1.21.3 cross model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/InventivetalentDev/minecraft-assets/1.21.3/assets/minecraft/models/block/cross.json) and [sunflower model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/InventivetalentDev/minecraft-assets/1.21.3/assets/minecraft/models/block/sunflower_top.json) to normalized triangle geometry. Stem planes meet at the full block height. The flower has separate front/back surfaces spaced 1/256 block apart. All three parts use the authored-model material so per-block grass scaling and tip bending cannot separate their joins.
